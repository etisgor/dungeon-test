Controls

Move
Tap the world to move. Tap a discovered site to travel there and open its interaction window.
Camera
Tap the camera icon to pause party-follow. Swipe the world to pan while follow is off.
Inventory
Open Character. Drag an item to an equipment or backpack slot, or tap an item and then its destination.
Journal
Open Journal for your current objective and expedition events.
Combat
Attacks resolve automatically. In assisted mode, tap Brace to reduce the next hit. Consumables are also in Character.
Cargo
Bring unsecured cargo back to the portal to store it. Defeat sends the party to the portal and loses unsecured cargo.
